MATHEMATICAL MODEL, PACK ODDS & MARKET DEPLETION FORMULAS

Exact binomial probability equations and post-release market aging adjustments powering the 2026 Topps Marvel Chrome & Neon calculator.

1 Binomial Complement Hit Probability Formula

For any character and parallel tier where the single-pack probability of pulling that specific card is p, the probability of pulling at least one copy across N packs is calculated using the binomial complement: 

P(At Least 1 Hit) = 1 - [ (1 - p_chrome_val)^N_cv × (1 - p_chrome_mega)^N_cm × (1 - p_chrome_hob)^N_ch × (1 - p_neon_val)^N_nv × (1 - p_neon_mega)^N_nm × (1 - p_neon_hob)^N_nh ]

Where p = 1 / (Pack_Odds × Checklist_Size). For example, on a 200-card base checklist, a parallel with 1:30 pack odds has a single-character per-pack probability of 1 / (30 × 200) = 1 / 6,000.

3 Post-Release Market Age & Loose-Box Depletion Model (Slider Math)

Static odds assume a Day 1 untouched print run where 100% of boxes remain sealed. Months after release, opening velocity and secondary-market seller behavior alter the effective odds of pulling top grails from loose boxes:

A. Exponential Global Supply Decay

Roughly 65% to 75% of modern Chrome/Neon wax is ripped within the first 60 to 90 days. With m representing months since release (λ = 0.35): 

Depletion(m) = 1 - e^(-0.35 × m)

At Month 3: ~65.0% ripped • Month 4: ~75.3% ripped • Month 6: ~87.8% ripped.

B. Loose-Box Adverse Selection ("Dead-Case Bias")

High-numbered parallels (/75 to /399) deplete evenly with boxes (α = 0). However, when a breaker or shop pulls a 1/1 SuperFractor or /5 early in a master case, the remaining "dead" boxes are often sold loose: 

p_adjusted = p_day1 × [ 1 - (α_tier × Depletion(m)) ]

Tier Bias (α): 1/1 = 0.45 • /5 = 0.28 • /10 = 0.20 • /25 = 0.10 • /50 = 0.04 • /75+ = 0.00.
